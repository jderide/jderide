# Julio Deride

Assistant Professor, Facultad de Ingeniería y Ciencias, Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez (Santiago, Chile).
Director of the MIIIO program (Magíster en Ingeniería Industrial e Investigación de Operaciones).
PhD in Applied Mathematics, UC Davis (advisor: Roger J-B Wets).

### Research

Stochastic optimization, variational analysis, and their applications to energy markets and equilibrium problems. Current lines:

- Rockafellian relaxation for optimization and equilibrium models
- Chance-constrained stochastic programming
- Applications to the Chilean electricity system (SEN)
