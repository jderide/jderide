## Projects

### Active Research

**Equilibrium of prices and holdings in continual restoration**
With Alejandro Jofré (CMM, U. Chile) and R.T. Rockafellar (U. Washington).
Building on [Deride, Jofré, Rockafellar (submitted)](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2305.17577) — extending the direct buying/selling equilibrium framework to dynamic settings.

**Pricing contingent claims under stochastic perturbations**
With Wolfgang Breytmann and Nicolás Hernández (USM).
Applying epi-convergence approximation theory to derivative pricing under model uncertainty.

**Likelihood-based estimation with covariance structures**
With L. Jiménez and M. Rosado (USM).
Developing optimization methods for structured covariance estimation problems.

### Completed Projects

**Stochastic equilibrium in incomplete financial markets** (2023)
Solo project — [arXiv:2308.05849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.05849). Computational framework for equilibrium problems in economies with incomplete financial instruments and retention constraints.

**CDF estimation under stochastic ambiguity** (2024)
With Johannes Royset (NPS) and Fernanda Urrea — [published in Set-Valued and Variational Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11228-024-00732-4). Variational approach to distribution estimation when the data-generating process is ambiguous. [Code available on GitHub](https://github.com/furrea/CDF-estimation).

**Stability and resilience of Chilean financial networks** (2023)
Funded by CMF Chile. Local stability analysis of interconnected financial networks with application to the Chilean banking system.

**Stochastic infrastructure planning for EV fast charging** (2016)
With Yueyue Fan and Zhaomiao Guo (UC Davis) — [published in Transportation Research Part C](http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2016.04.010). Competitive market model for optimal placement of EV charging stations under demand uncertainty.

**Probabilistic scenario generation for solar/wind power** (2018)
With David Woodruff, Andrea Staid et al. (Sandia National Laboratories) — [published in Solar Energy](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0038092X17310605). Methodology for constructing scenario trees representing forecast errors in wide-area renewable generation.
